# Article titles and deks

Read the full article, available strategy, intended reader and voice before
writing. Use the provided file or pasted article. Ask for an article when absent,
and ask for the audience only when it cannot be recovered. State the central
insight, audience and voice briefly.

## Write the thought first

Extract the article's central insight, reader concern, specific evidence and
unspoken implication. Draft titles as complete thoughts. An inversion should
live in the sentence itself, not in an attached technique label. Prefer plain
words that sound natural spoken aloud. Strip adjectives and adverbs, then
restore only words the meaning needs. Do not invent figures or amplify a claim
past the article's evidence.

Generate five distinct title and dek pairs. Title: at most 12 words, ideally
four to eight. Dek: one sentence of at most 20 words. Combined: at most 32 words.
The dek confirms the value behind the title without merely summarizing the body.

After drafting, use [the technique reference](reference/techniques.md) to diagnose
repetition and vary the batch. Read [the wording checks](reference/humanizer-blacklist.md)
for the final pass. [The synthetic examples](reference/reference-titles.md) train
the ear; they are not client drafts, quotations or templates to fill in.

## Review

Read each title aloud. Does it sound like a person making an interesting claim?
Check that it makes sense without the article underneath and remains legible as
a social cover. Remove clickbait, unsupported urgency, jargon, repeated technique
shapes and luxury filler. Honor the current brief's restrictions on brand names.
Compare the strongest option with the existing title; recommend keeping the old
one when it is better. Do not pad the batch with a claim the article cannot prove.

## Deliver

For a supplied file, replace only its `## Title Options` section and preserve the
existing title, article, hooks and unrelated edits. For pasted text without a
destination, return the options in chat. Number each pair, give its diagnostic
label, title and dek, then recommend one with a sentence explaining why. Include
the existing title for comparison when present and return any saved file path.

The optional `hooks` skill writes opening lines; `creative` builds HTML creatives.
This skill writes titles and deks and does not publish the article.
