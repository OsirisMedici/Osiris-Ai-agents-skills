# Synthetic title examples

These are invented examples for calibration, not client drafts, verified claims,
campaign results or quotations. Use only claims supported by the actual article.

- The shortcut made the queue longer.
- Three missing fields stopped the launch.
- The faster printer became the bottleneck.
- What the checklist forgot.
- I optimized the wrong step.
- The quietest change saved the morning.

Notice how nouns and verbs carry the thought. Read the candidate aloud, check
its evidence and ask whether it still makes sense without the body beneath it.
