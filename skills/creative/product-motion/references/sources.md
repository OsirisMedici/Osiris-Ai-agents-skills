## Examples and tools

- [Component gallery](https://animations.dev/gallery): compare toasts, drawers, tabs, shared elements and multi-step states. Public demos do not establish access to implementation lessons.
- [Easings](https://easings.co/): experiment with cubic-bezier curves. Preserve project tokens unless a change improves the interaction.

## Access and interpretation

Easing and transitions text, vocabulary, gallery and changelog were readable during the original checks. Some embedded media did not play. The timing lesson and custom easing collection required login; the skills page requested email signup. No paid course, private vault or packaged skill was retrieved. Access can change; this is a dated observation, not a current-access guarantee.

The numerical defaults and our use cases require judgment. The author's preferences for custom curves are aesthetic guidance. Older performance examples describe particular implementations and do not establish the rendering behavior of current Motion versions.
