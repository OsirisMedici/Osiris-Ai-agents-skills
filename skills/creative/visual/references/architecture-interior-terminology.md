# Architecture & interior terminology

For Osiris's core client work — architects, interior designers, luxury residential firms. This is where "generic AI render" becomes "editorial-grade photograph."

## Materials (the biggest tell)

AI image models drift toward generic "marble + wood + metal." Naming specific materials with brand-level specificity pulls the output into professional territory.

### Stone
- **Travertine** — cream, porous, banded, Italian classic
- **Marble (Carrara)** — white with grey veining, most common
- **Marble (Calacatta)** — white with bold gold/brown veining, high-end
- **Marble (Verde Alpi)** — deep green with white veining
- **Marble (Nero Marquina)** — black with stark white veining
- **Onyx** — translucent, backlit glow, luxury bar/bath
- **Limestone** — matte, pale, warm grey, English country / modernist
- **Terrazzo** — composite, chips in cement, 70s revival
- **Sandstone** — warm tan, rough texture, desert/earthy
- **Slate** — dark grey, cleft surface, moody
- **Basalt** — deep near-black volcanic stone, Japanese
- **Quartzite** — crystalline, harder than marble

### Wood
- **Walnut (American black)** — chocolate brown, open grain, warm
- **White oak (rift-sawn)** — pale cream, straight grain, minimalist
- **White oak (fumed / smoked)** — grey-brown, modernized
- **Teak** — warm golden, weathers silver outdoors, mid-century / yachty
- **Ash (pale/blond)** — near-white, Scandinavian
- **Mahogany** — deep reddish-brown, colonial / classic
- **Ebony** — near-black, exotic, bold contrast
- **Reclaimed barn wood** — weathered grey-brown, distressed
- **Cerused oak** — limed white, open grain filled white, Parisian

### Metal
- **Brushed brass** — warm, matte, 60s-90s revival, hospitality
- **Polished brass** — reflective, Art Deco, glamorous
- **Blackened steel** — dark grey-black, industrial, architectural
- **Patinated copper** — green/blue tarnish, antique
- **Chrome (polished)** — mirror-like, cold, 70s / 80s revival
- **Nickel (satin)** — cool silver-grey, American classic
- **Bronze** — warm brown-gold, patinated variations
- **Aluminum (brushed)** — matte silver, modern tech
- **Cor-ten steel** — rust-orange, architectural façade

### Plaster and render
- **Limewash** — chalky, textured, Mediterranean / rustic chic
- **Venetian plaster** — polished, mottled, luxurious
- **Tadelakt (Moroccan)** — waxed lime plaster, seamless, bath
- **Roman clay** — matte, earthy, textured
- **Microcement** — seamless modern, pale to dark
- **Raw unfinished plaster** — pale grey-pink, brutalist chic

### Concrete
- **Raw (formwork-marked) concrete** — brutalist, rough, grey
- **Polished concrete** — glossy, reflective, industrial lofts
- **Tinted concrete** — pigmented, earth tones
- **Board-formed concrete** — imprinted wood grain, Tadao Ando

### Textile
- **Bouclé** — looped, creamy, trend 2020+ (Pierre Paulin chairs)
- **Linen** — woven, relaxed, natural, Kinfolk
- **Velvet (silk / cotton / mohair)** — plush, luxury, jewel tones
- **Wool / tweed** — textured, English country / tailored
- **Raw silk** — subtle sheen, Japanese refinement
- **Linen slipcovers** — casual European, washed and rumpled
- **Mohair** — hairy texture, soft, mid-century
- **Leather (full-grain)** — natural, patinating, timeless
- **Cognac leather** — warm tan, aged
- **Jute / sisal rugs** — natural fiber, casual / coastal

### Glass and transparencies
- **Fluted glass** — ribbed, privacy + modernism
- **Mouth-blown cylinder glass** — imperfect, heritage
- **Smoked glass** — brown/grey tinted, 70s revival
- **Mirror (antiqued)** — foxed, decorative
- **Crystal** — cut, glittering, classical

## Architectural styles

| Style | Visual markers |
|---|---|
| **Brutalist** | Raw concrete, bold geometry, massive forms, minimal ornament |
| **Minimalist (Japandi)** | Pale wood, white walls, low furniture, empty space, Japanese/Scandi fusion |
| **Modernist (International Style)** | Flat roof, horizontal lines, large windows, white/glass/steel |
| **Mid-century modern** | Walnut, tapered legs, organic curves, 50s-60s American |
| **Art Deco** | Brass, marble, geometric patterns, black + gold + ivory |
| **Bauhaus** | Primary colors, geometric, functionalist, tubular steel |
| **Mediterranean (modern)** | Limewash, arches, terracotta, natural materials, Aegean / Provençal |
| **Wabi-Sabi** | Imperfect, weathered, earthy, patinated, Japanese tea-room |
| **Maximalist** | Dense layering, bold patterns, rich colors, saturation |
| **French Provincial** | Aged oak, soft palette, toile, distressed, countryside refined |
| **Industrial loft** | Exposed brick, steel beams, polished concrete, large factory windows |
| **Scandinavian** | Pale wood, white, functional, hygge, soft textiles |
| **English Country** | Floral chintz, antique wood, books, patinated brass |
| **Coastal (Hamptons / Amalfi)** | White, blue, woven, weathered timber, rattan |
| **Tropical Modernism** | Geoffrey Bawa, open plan, tropical plants, pale walls, teak, water |

## Spatial qualities

- **Volume** — ceiling height, vertical breathing room
- **Cantilever** — structural overhang, no visible support
- **Enfilade** — doors aligned through multiple rooms, classical
- **Light wells / skylights** — shafts of daylight punching down
- **Double-height space** — two-story volume, drama
- **Interior courtyard** — open to sky within footprint
- **Sliding (pocket) doors** — clean integration, Japanese / modern
- **Inglenook fireplace** — recessed seating around hearth
- **Clerestory window** — high band of windows, light without view
- **Jib door / concealed door** — flush with wall, hidden entry

## Staging moods

- **Editorial untouched** — nothing out of place, styled for photography, cold perfection
- **Lived-in** — books stacked, throw blanket crumpled, coffee on table
- **Showroom** — perfectly merchandised, retail gloss
- **Atelier** — artist's / designer's working studio, textures and samples
- **Hotel suite** — polished, generous, fresh flowers
- **Private residence (luxury)** — personal objects, art collection, warmth
- **Time-of-day staging** — morning (coffee, light blanket), evening (wine, dim lamps)

## Era markers (for the `atmosphere.era` field)

- **1920s-30s Art Deco**: geometric, brass, black lacquer, jazz luxury
- **1940s-50s Mid-Century**: walnut, atomic, Eames, earnest optimism
- **1960s**: Op Art, bold colors, Pierre Paulin, sculptural
- **1970s**: browns, terracotta, bouclé, Saarinen, conversation pits
- **1980s Memphis**: primary colors, pattern, postmodern play
- **1990s minimalism**: stripped, Calvin Klein interiors, pale wood
- **2000s-10s**: open plan, grey, glass, smart-home
- **Contemporary (2020s)**: curves, mushroom shapes, tactile, bouclé revival, warm minimalism

## Signature moves catalog (steal these)

Every polished architectural photo has 2-3 specific moves. Examples to pull from:

- A single shaft of sunlight cutting diagonally across a polished concrete floor
- A brass lamp as the only warm element in a cool-lit room
- Ceiling-height curtains that pool on the floor
- A single stem flower in a raw ceramic vase on an empty table
- A stack of 3-5 books with a reading light on a side table
- An open book or magazine face-down on an arm of a sofa
- An abstract artwork as the only color in an otherwise monochrome room
- A half-drawn curtain letting a knife-edge of light through
- Bare feet on a linen throw on a bouclé sofa (lifestyle editorial)
- A steam rising from a mug on a low wood table
- Long shadows from a window grille across a limewash wall
- A solitary figure in the background, out of focus, barely visible
- Overlapping runner rugs — pattern over pattern
- A door left slightly ajar, showing another room beyond
- Candle flame reflected in a polished surface
- Wet footprints on a stone bathroom floor (hotel editorial)
- A curtain caught mid-billow by breeze through an open window
- Fresh flowers mid-arrangement (not finished) on a kitchen island
- Fire in a fireplace as only color source in a B&W-leaning image
- A paint-splattered worktop (artist's atelier)
