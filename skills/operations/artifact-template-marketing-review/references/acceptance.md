## Evidence and human checks

Complete the checks below in the audit report; formatting checks cannot establish them:

- Verify live account/location identities and matching registered read access. No account-name or tool-namespace guess substitutes for IDs. Keep CRM/ad operations read-only during reporting.
- Verify source period, account timezone, extraction/evidence cutoffs and test exclusion. Use actual enquiry timestamps. A selected project-intent cohort is not all paid submissions or sales-confirmed qualifications.
- Inspect the actual manual note source records. Workflow messages and generated summaries are excluded. Manual edits to the destination are preserved, even if they differ from an older export; flag the conflict instead of overwriting them.
- Compare every project question against the client mapping. Preserve budget units, raw answers, contradictions and missing fields. Do not compare cost per room with a total project budget.
- Review fit predictions and the single blocker against the client's criteria and the actual enquiry. A plausible name/language alone does not establish spam. Keep possible spam provisional and never mutate CRM qualification.
- Check useful campaign-level budget/scope/vacancy/location/missing-detail patterns and denominators. Keep generated actions concise and within media-buyer responsibilities. Sales ownership, founder meetings, proposals, payment and vendor handling remain outside this report.
- Verify original media identity, hashes/version and actual reuse. Include non-converting ads when a full campaign review is requested; a selected-cohort test may explicitly limit archives to originating ads. Do not silently narrow the archive scope. Show poster-only video limitations and retain historical versions.
- Inspect all three components in the visible in-app Browser, the longest ad names/answers and full note text when selected. Check that compact previews preserve full cells and remain usable. Preserve actual human cell notes/threaded comments and avoid generated boilerplate.
- Compare before/after values, formulas, links, actual notes, colour rules and source tabs. An authorized generated-annotation cleanup may change only those annotation fields. Do not alter values, timezone behaviour, sharing or the approved reference reference during a skill-only audit.

Record pass, observed output gap, expected client-data difference or unavailable evidence for every applicable check. State unresolved items plainly. Use historical snapshots as historical evidence, not fresh CRM/Meta state.
