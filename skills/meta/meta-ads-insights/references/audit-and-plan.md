# Campaign review and next launch

## Establish the outcome

Identify who should enquire or register, the offer, dates, delivery mode, fee and desired conversion. Confirm only details that materially change the plan. Retrieve the actual offer and destination first; do not ask the user for discoverable account facts. Ask once for a budget ceiling or registration target when absent, then label useful scenarios while continuing the audit.

## Inventory the live hierarchy

Read the campaign and its ad sets, published ads and relevant creatives. Capture IDs, parent relationships, configured/effective status, start dates, objective, budget level and currency, bid strategy, optimization event, destination and targeting. Include the actual geographic scope, age, placements, audience automation, interests, custom audiences and exclusions where returned. Distinguish missing settings from broad targeting.

Reconcile each ad set's published ad count. An active ad set can contain no published ads. Draft-reading support can be unavailable during rollout; report that limitation instead of claiming that missing published objects do not exist as drafts.

Inspect rendered creatives after their media loads. Compare hooks, offer details, trainer identity, proof, CTA, legibility and placement suitability. Separate creative review from measured creative performance. Similar color treatments or repeated layouts do not establish audience tests. Inspect alternate copy and enhancement variants for claim drift, even when the base body is accurate.

## Compare performance at a fair grain

Use a clearly dated completed-day window in the account's reporting timezone. For newly started ads, add today's partial window separately and label it. Use the campaign's available history when the initial window predates its start. Record actual dates for presets when returned; do not invent a lifetime start date.

Request spend, impressions, reach, link clicks, landing-page views, the exact lead/result type and cost per result where available. Add diagnostics that can change the decision. Reconcile ad totals to the campaign for additive metrics before comparing. Use the same attribution settings and conversion event for comparisons or disclose the mismatch.

- Cost per lead equals spend divided by the matching reported lead count. An unavailable or zero denominator has no finite CPL.
- Link CTR uses link clicks divided by impressions. It differs from CTR across all clicks.
- Qualified-lead cost and registration acquisition cost require matched CRM/payment outcomes. Do not label platform leads qualified or calculate return on ad spend from the workshop fee alone.
- A campaign created today with zero reported spend and unavailable results has no performance winner. Active status and a working connection do not demonstrate delivery.
- Unequal delivery and small samples limit creative comparisons. Describe a directional candidate with its spend and lead count; do not claim causal uplift from an uncontrolled comparison.

## Follow the actual destination

Extract every scoped ad's destination, including link data or asset overrides. Open the actual URL and the intended landing page when they differ. Do not assume a newly built page receives paid traffic.

Capture and inspect screenshots of the hero, primary CTA destination, form's initial state and material offer/terms sections. Check mobile rendering when the supported browser can provide it; disclose when only desktop or placement previews were checked. Do not submit a fake enquiry to complete a read-only audit.

Compare ad and page promises for dates, fee and final-tax wording, scope, delivery mode, recordings, support, instructor and CTA. Qualify unconfirmed syllabus, prerequisites, commercial terms and technical outcomes. A creative promising registration and a page offering a suitability enquiry can create different expectations.

Inspect tracking configuration when accessible, including the optimized pixel/event and URL tags. A survey URL parameter such as `notrack=true` merits investigation; its name alone does not prove tracking is broken. Separate a configured Lead event from a verified completed submission. Identify the exact test needed to prove event firing, deduplication, CRM receipt and attribution, with user approval before a test that creates a record or sends a message.

## Prepare the launch proposal

Use the evidence to propose the smallest campaign structure that answers the business need. Explain audience and conversion tradeoffs. Avoid copying every old ad set or fragmenting a small budget into many overlapping tests. Separate materially different offers, locations or delivery modes when the budget and outcome justify it. Treat retargeting as conditional on a usable audience; included custom audiences may be suggestions under an automated audience setup, so inspect the actual controls before promising a strict warm audience.

Provide reviewable campaign/ad set names, audience definitions and exclusions, conversion goal, dates, placement/format plan, distinct creative concepts and draft primary text, headlines and CTAs. Keep local offer facts separate from this generic skill. Do not manufacture urgency, guarantees, certificates, job outcomes, discounts or syllabus claims.

Show the budget period, total cap, initial test allocation and conditions for releasing more. Separate media from production, fees and applicable taxes. When measured CPL is unavailable, use labeled sensitivities rather than an invented forecast. Derive affordability from an explicit acquisition-cost ceiling and conversion assumptions. Revenue alone does not establish profitable acquisition cost.

Specify the next decision rule in terms of lead count, spend, quality and tracking validity. Finish with the exact gaps that prevent launch or reliable optimization. Keep the proposal saved in the authorized task workspace and leave live delivery unchanged unless the user separately authorizes specific changes.
